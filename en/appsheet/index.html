Who it's for

Who is a system built on AppSheet for?

  • You're used to the Google ecosystem, don't want to spend a lot of effort learning something new, and don't want a painful switch to a new system.
  • You'd like to buy a system outright, not pay for a subscription every year.
  • You want to own your data, and be free to download and move it.
  • You care about security, and since Google is a big company, you find it more trustworthy.
  • The system has about 1 to 10 users. (With more people, the system can get complicated, and AppSheet may not be the right way to build it.)

Features

What is AppSheet? Features

  • Data connections and views: Connect Google Sheets or Excel and get forms, charts, dashboards and other mobile-friendly screens.
  • Offline use and sync: Enter data offline; it syncs automatically once you're back online.
  • User management: Basic permission control, so you can share it with the people you work with.
  • Works across devices: Computer browsers, Android and iOS phones, tablets and more.
