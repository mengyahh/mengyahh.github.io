title: A "Japanese Food" Themed Dinner
---

![](202406-1-1.webp)

- **Nanbanzuke**: Fry the fish, then soak it in a sweet and sour sauce! Later when I ate the supermarket version in Japan, mine turned out very similar!
- **Iberico sausage salad**: The classic olive oil + balsamic vinegar
- **Pan-fried green beans and shishito peppers**
- Store-bought corn dumplings turned into potstickers. Sadly I couldn't find the really tasty vegetarian dumplings.
- **Chicken hearts and gizzards**: A drinking snack, and the crispy garlic slices at the end are the soul of it!
- **Okra with salted kelp**: Add sesame oil for a salty flavor, a side dish I've always loved!
- **Tsukudani smelt**: There was nothing to pair it with in this meal, so it was a bit salty.
- **Tazukuri (walnut and dried baby sardines)**: A Japanese New Year dish
- **Smoked salmon salad**: A refreshing lemon flavor
