- **Crispy egg crepe (dan bing)**: I found a small shop near the guesthouse that serves Taiwanese breakfast, and after eating there I realized... I couldn't let my foreign friends have a real crispy dan bing, so I made it for them myself! I kneaded the dough (my dough-kneading experience is probably under three times), and it was a super successful re-creation!
- **Taiwanese-style pickles**: Very simple ingredients: napa cabbage, with vinegar and sugar 1:1. It goes super well with traditional Japanese pickled plums that are salty to the point of exploding, but you need to soak it longer before the plum flavor comes out (maybe four or five days?). I left a little behind when I left, and later the German guy even messaged me specially to say it was super good once the plum flavor came through!
- **Apple cake**: It's all thanks to pancake mix! But I deserve a round of applause for making it in a small stainless steel pot XD Because it was thick, it had to be cooked on low heat (in fact it burned, and I cut off a thin layer, but once I laid the apples on top it looked like it had always been that way XD)
- **Fried foods**: This time I fried on an induction stove, and practiced how the temperature is actually different for different ingredients. For example, vegetables need a relatively lower temperature, while potatoes and sweet potatoes need a higher one. Vegetables first, then meat. The Japanese guests praised my frying! (Even if it was just politeness, that's fine! So happy!)
