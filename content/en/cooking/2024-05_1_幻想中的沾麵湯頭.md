title: The Dipping Noodle Broth of My Imagination
---

![](202405-1-1.webp)

- The broth is based on chicken (chicken stock) and kombu. There should have been Japanese soy sauce and mirin, though I'm not sure. At the end I added a stronger, beany miso (which also makes the broth thicker and clings better to the noodles), plus fragrant white sesame.
- Besides chicken (I used thigh this time, but I'd rather use wings to add more collagen and make the broth thicker), there were also carrots, carrots and shiitake.
- My friends praised it enthusiastically!
