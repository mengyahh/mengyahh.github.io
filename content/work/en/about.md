::: process How we work
## How we do it together

Get to know and understand first, then talk it through and think about how to do it.

### Let's talk
A one-hour first chat. Tell me what you're thinking and what you need.

### Get on site
I watch how things are actually done, look at the data you already have, and talk to the people involved.

### Find the shape of it
We sort the problems by priority and decide together what to do and how far to go.

### Build it together
I take responsibility for the work, but you can't just hand everything over and walk away. I need your help and ideas too — after all, you know your own situation better than I do.

### Hand it over
I hate waste, and I hate the idea of taking your money and making something you can't use. I'll walk you through it and hand it over properly.

---

Just want to get the problem straight? That's fine too!
You can hire me for steps 2 and 3 only, the assessment and planning. Sometimes it turns out the plan isn't workable and the goal needs adjusting, and sometimes it turns out it's something I can't do. That's okay — hand it to someone more suitable.
:::
