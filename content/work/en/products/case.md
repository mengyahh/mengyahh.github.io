The **Case Management System** is used to record the information of the cases you serve. Besides keeping track of all the case data, it lets the organisation review its yearly and monthly service figures and assess its social impact.

::: features
### Track all case data
All case data lives in one system, so you can follow everything.

### Opening and closing
Record when a case is opened and when it is closed.

### Personal details
Record the case's personal details.

### Services and interventions
Record the services and interventions each case receives.

### Placement
Record the placement period, location and other details.

### Service overview and social impact
The organisation can review its yearly and monthly service figures, and assess its social impact.
:::
